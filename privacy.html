Privacy Notice

How DUNIZ handles shared information.

Transparency on information shared through the DUNIZ website and enquiry form.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who we are

DUNIZ Business Consultants is a strategic business advisory practice based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. You can contact us at anupam@duniz.com.

Information you provide

When you contact DUNIZ using this site or your email application, you may provide your name, work email, organization, advisory area and a description of your business enquiry.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to understand the subject of your request, respond to you and, if you choose to proceed, discuss a potential professional engagement. We do not publish your enquiry or sell your contact information.

Delivery services

When the website’s direct enquiry service is active, submissions are transmitted to an email-delivery service to forward them to our business inbox. Until that service is configured, the form offers an email option using your own email application.

External resources

This website loads typography from Google Fonts, which may receive ordinary technical requests when the site is visited. Standard hosting and security systems may process technical request logs.

Retention and requests

Enquiry records are retained only as reasonably necessary for responding, keeping legitimate business records and meeting applicable requirements. To ask about information you have shared, request correction, or request deletion where applicable, email anupam@duniz.com.

Changes

This notice may be updated as our website services evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
